      Flight controls

      Fly
      Tap Space to jump. Hold Space in the air or the mouse button to fly.
      Arcade deck
      Drag Steer, or focus it and use arrows, to steer and change depth. Tap Jump / Fly to jump or hold it to fly. Press or hold Fire to shoot. Use Start or Play Again to begin a run. Space and Enter activate the focused action.
      Boss arena
      Hold Space or Fly to rise during combat. Watch the boss wind-up and incoming attack status.
      Descend
      Hold Down or S for a small boost while airborne. Lift takes priority.
      Road depth
      Up/W and Down/S move along road depth while grounded. Q/E step between lanes.
      Steer
      Left / Right or A / D.
      Shoot
      Hold F or right mouse. Tap or hold Fire on touch.. All shots share the same cooldown.
      Pause
      P or Escape.
      Touch
      Tap Fly to jump or hold it to rise. Drag on the left to steer. Tap or hold Fire.
